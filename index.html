04

General Requirements

4.1 Impartiality  ·  4.2 Confidentiality

Clause 4.1 · Impartiality

Impartiality

ClauseRequirementEvidence / To Consider
4.1.1 Laboratory activities shall be undertaken impartially — i.e. presence of bias or conflicts of interest is not permitted. Policy statements, top-level commitment
4.1.2 Does the laboratory demonstrate management commitment to impartiality? Management review · Code of conduct · Declaration forms relating to confidentiality / impartiality
4.1.3 Top management has overall responsibility for impartiality of the laboratory's activities. Organisation chart, job descriptions, policy sign-off
4.1.4 Does the laboratory identify risks to its impartiality on an ongoing basis? Risk from activities · Risk from its relationships · Risk from the relationships of its personnel
To consider: Risk assessment · Risk matrix
4.1.5 Does the laboratory eliminate or minimize the risks identified? Action plans, mitigation records, review of effectiveness
Assessor focus: Impartiality risks are a living process — not a one-off declaration. Expect to see evidence of ongoing identification, mitigation and review.
Clause 4.2 · Confidentiality

Confidentiality

ClauseRequirementEvidence / To Consider
4.2.1 How is the laboratory responsible for the confidentiality of information obtained or created during laboratory activities, and for the proprietary rights of its customers? Code of conduct · Contractual agreement
4.2.2 Does the laboratory notify its customers when it is legally obliged to release confidential information? Notification records, contract clauses
4.2.3 Is information about customers received from sources other than the customer kept confidential? Is the source not revealed to the customer unless agreed by the source? Confidentiality procedure, staff briefings
4.2.4 How does the laboratory ensure that all personnel (including contracted personnel) keep confidential all information obtained or created during laboratory activities? Signed confidentiality agreements, induction records, contracts with subcontracted personnel
Remember: Confidentiality obligations extend beyond employees — contracted, seconded and temporary personnel are all in scope.

Clause 6.5 · Metrological Traceability

Metrological traceability

ClauseRequirementEvidence / To Consider
6.5.1 Does the laboratory establish and maintain metrological traceability of its measurement results by means of a documented unbroken chain of calibrations, each contributing to the measurement uncertainty, linking them to an appropriate reference? Traceability charts, calibration certificates, uncertainty statements
6.5.2 Does the laboratory ensure that measurement results are traceable to the International System (SI) of Units where possible? Calibration certificates from NMI / accredited labs
6.5.3 Where SI traceability is not possible, the laboratory demonstrates traceability to an appropriate reference (e.g. certified reference materials, consensus standards, specified methods). To consider:
· Calibration provided by a competent laboratory
· Certified reference materials by a competent producer
· Direct realization of SI units
Assessor focus: Traceability is not just a certificate on file — the laboratory must be able to show the chain and how each link contributes to the reported measurement uncertainty.

Process Requirements

7.1 – 7.11  ·  The technical heart of the standard

Clause 7.1 · Review of Requests, Tenders & Contracts

Review of requests, tenders and contracts

  • 7.1.1 — Does the laboratory have a procedure for the review of requests, tenders and contracts? The review ensures that:
    • Requirements are adequately defined, documented and understood
    • The laboratory has the capability and resources to meet them
    • The appropriate method is selected and capable of meeting the customer's requirements
  • 7.1.2 — Does the laboratory:
    • Notify the customer of any inappropriate or outdated methods, and ensure the contract is acceptable to both parties?
    • Ensure that deviations requested by the customer do not impact the integrity of the laboratory or the validity of the results?
    • Ensure the decision rule is clearly defined, communicated and agreed by both parties when a statement of conformity is required?
    • Notify customers of any deviations from the contract and, if these occur after work has commenced, repeat the contract review and notify all affected personnel?
  • 7.1.3 — Does the laboratory cooperate with customers or their representatives in clarifying the customer's request and in monitoring the laboratory's performance in relation to the work performed?
  • 7.1.4 — Selection, verification and validation of methods (see 7.2).
To produce: Records of contract review · Records of any changes and pertinent discussions on customer's requirements.

7.2.1.3Does the laboratory select and inform the customer of the method chosen if the customer does not specify the method?Contract records, customer communications
7.2.1.4Does the laboratory verify that it can properly perform the methods and achieve the required performance before introducing them?To produce: Records of verification
7.2.1.5Has the laboratory verified that it can properly perform the methods and achieve the required performance before introducing them?To produce: Records of verification
7.2.1.6Does the laboratory have a formal plan for method development, performed by competent personnel with adequate resources? Are periodic reviews carried out to ensure that the needs of the customer are fulfilled?Method development plan, review records
7.2.1.7Does the laboratory document, justify, authorize and seek acceptance from the customer for any deviations to methods prior to performing them?Deviation records, customer acceptance

Clause 7.3 – 7.4

Sampling & handling of test or calibration items

7.3 Sampling

  • 7.3.1 — Does the laboratory have a sampling plan and method for performing sampling activities?
    • Is the sampling plan and method available at the site where sampling is performed?
    • Does the sampling method address the factors to be controlled to ensure the validity of subsequent testing or calibration results?
  • 7.3.2 — Does the laboratory record sampling data as part of the laboratory activity?
  • 7.3.3 — Are deviations from the sampling plan/method recorded and reported?
To produce: Sampling method describing the plan, the method and the factors to be controlled.

7.4 Handling of test or calibration items

ClauseRequirementEvidence / To Produce
7.4.1Does the laboratory have a system for unambiguous identification of test or calibration items that ensures no physical confusion or when referred to in records or documents? Is the identification retained whilst under the responsibility of the laboratory?Item identification procedure, labels
7.4.2Does the laboratory take precautions to avoid deterioration, contamination, loss or damage to the item during handling, transport, storage, preparation and retention?Handling & storage procedures
7.4.3Upon receipt of items which have deviated from specified conditions, does the laboratory record these deviations? Are the deviations notified to customers for their instructions and the results of this consultation recorded?To produce: Records for the above
7.4.4For items requiring storage under specific environmental conditions, does the laboratory maintain, monitor and record these conditions?Storage condition logs
Clause 7.5 – 7.6

Technical records & evaluation of measurement uncertainty

7.5 Technical records

ClauseRequirementEvidence / To Produce
7.5.1 Does the laboratory retain and maintain technical records containing sufficient information to enable repetition of the laboratory activity under conditions as close as possible to the original?
  • Date and identity of personnel responsible for the laboratory activity and checking of data and results
  • Factors affecting measurement results and its associated measurement uncertainty
  • Evaluation of measurement uncertainty
 To produce: Records for the above containing (but not limited to) the listed items
7.5.2 Are amendments to technical reports trackable to previous versions or original observations? Are the original and amended data and files kept?
  • Date of alteration
  • Indication of altered aspects
  • Personnel responsible for the alterations
 To produce: Records for the above

7.6 Evaluation of measurement uncertainty

  • 7.6.1 — Does the laboratory identify the contributions to measurement uncertainty using appropriate methods of analysis, including those arising from sampling?
  • 7.6.2 — For laboratories performing calibrations, including of its own equipment, is the measurement uncertainty for all calibrations evaluated?
  • 7.6.3 — For laboratories performing testing, has the measurement uncertainty been evaluated?
    NB: If it is not possible to evaluate the measurement uncertainty, an estimation of the uncertainty must be made.

Clause 7.8.1 – 7.8.2 · Reporting of Results

Reporting of results — general & common requirements

  • 7.8.1.1 — Does the laboratory review and authorize the results prior to release? Are any results not reported to the customer made readily available?

7.8.2.1 — Does the report contain at least the following information?

  • a) A title (e.g. "Test Report", "Calibration Certificate" or "Report of Sampling")
  • b) Name and address of the laboratory
  • c) Location of performance of the laboratory activities (including customer facility, sites away, temporary or mobile facilities)
  • d) Unique identification that all components are recognized as a portion of a complete report, and a clear identification of the end
  • e) Name and contact information of the customer
  • f) Identification of the method used
  • g) A description, unambiguous identification, and, when necessary, the condition of the item
  • h) Date of receipt of the item(s) and the date of sampling, where critical to validity
  • i) Date(s) of performance of the laboratory activity
  • j) Date of issue of the report
  • k) Reference to the sampling plan and sampling method used, where relevant
  • l) A statement that the results relate only to the items tested, calibrated or sampled
  • m) The results with, where appropriate, the units of measurement
  • n) Additions to, deviations, or exclusions from the method
  • o) Identification of the person(s) authorizing the report
  • p) Clear identification when results are from external providers
7.8.2.2 — When applicable, does the laboratory clearly identify in its reports which information and/or sample is provided by the customer?
Clause 7.8.3 – 7.8.5 · Specific Requirements

Test, calibration & sampling reports

7.8.3 — Specific requirements for test reports

Does the test report contain the following where necessary?

  • a) Information on specific test conditions, such as environmental conditions
  • b) Where relevant, a statement of conformity with requirements or specifications
  • c) Where applicable, the measurement uncertainty presented in the same unit as the measurand, or in a term relative to the measurand (e.g. percent), when:
    — it is relevant to the validity or application of the test results;
    — a customer's instruction so requires; or
    — the measurement uncertainty affects conformity to a specification limit
  • d) Where appropriate, opinions and interpretations
  • e) Additional information which may be required by specific methods, authorities, customers or groups of customers

7.8.4 — Specific requirements for calibration reports

  • a) The measurement uncertainty of the measurement result, presented in the same unit as the measurand or in a term relative to the measurand (e.g. percent)
  • The conditions (e.g. environmental) under which the calibrations were made that have an influence on the measurement results
  • A statement identifying how the measurements are metrologically traceable
  • The results before and after any adjustment or repair, if available
  • Where relevant, a statement of conformity with requirements or specifications
  • Where appropriate, opinions and interpretations
  • 7.8.4.3 — Does the calibration laboratory ensure that certificates or labels do not contain any recommendation on the calibration interval? If so, has this been agreed with the customer?

7.8.5 — Specific requirements for sampling reports

  • The date of sampling
  • Unique identification of the item or material sampled (including manufacturer name, model/type designation and serial numbers as appropriate)
  • The location of sampling, including any diagrams, sketches or photographs
  • A reference to the sampling plan and sampling method
  • Details of any environmental conditions during sampling that affect the interpretation of results
  • Information required to evaluate measurement uncertainty for subsequent testing or calibration
Clause 7.8.6 – 7.8.8

Statements of conformity, opinions & amendments

7.8.6 — Reporting statements of conformity

  • 7.8.6.1 — Does the laboratory document the decision rule employed when making statements of conformity in the report?
  • 7.8.6.2 — When reporting on the statement of conformity, does the statement clearly identify:
    a) to which results the statement of conformity applies?
    b) which specifications, standards or parts thereof are met or not met?
    c) the decision rule applied?

7.8.7 — Reporting opinions and interpretations

  • 7.8.7.1 — Does the laboratory ensure that opinions and interpretations are made and released by authorized personnel? Does the laboratory document the basis on which the opinions and interpretations are made?
  • 7.8.7.2 — Is it clearly identifiable in the reports when the laboratory makes opinions and interpretations based on the results obtained from the tested or calibrated item?
  • 7.8.7.3 — Does the laboratory keep records of any communications of opinions and interpretations with the customer?

7.8.8 — Amendments to reports

  • 7.8.8.1 — When the laboratory makes any changes to a report, is this clearly identified in the report? Where appropriate, are the reasons for the changes clearly identified?
  • 7.8.8.2 — When changes are made to a report after it has been issued, does the laboratory issue it as a further document which includes the statement "Amendment to report, serial number" or other equivalent form of wording?
  • 7.8.8.3 — When issuing a completely new report, does the laboratory issue a unique identification to this report and include a reference to the original report?

Clause 7.10 – 7.11

Nonconforming work & control of data and information management

7.10 — Nonconforming work

  • 7.10.1 — Does the laboratory have a procedure for dealing with nonconforming work?
    • a) Does the procedure ensure that the responsibilities and authorities for the management of nonconforming work are defined?
    • b) Are actions (including halting work, withholding results) taken based on the significance of the nonconformity?
    • c) Is the customer notified and, where necessary, are results recalled?
    • d) Is the significance of the nonconforming work evaluated and appropriate action taken?
    • e) Are records retained of nonconforming work, actions taken and the results of any corrective action?

7.11 — Control of data and information management

  • 7.11.1 — Does the laboratory have access to the data and information needed to perform laboratory activities?
  • 7.11.2 — Are information systems validated, protected, maintained and controlled to ensure the integrity of data?
  • 7.11.3 — Does the laboratory ensure that calculations and data transfers are checked in an appropriate and systematic manner? Is the system able to record system failures and the appropriate immediate and corrective actions?
  • 7.11.4 — Does the laboratory ensure that external providers or the operator of the LIMS complies with the requirements of Section 7.11 if it is managed offsite?
  • 7.11.5 — Does the laboratory ensure that instructions, manuals and reference data relevant to the laboratory information management system(s) are made readily available to personnel?
  • 7.11.6 — Does the laboratory ensure that calculations and data transfers are checked in an appropriate and systematic manner?

Management System Requirements

8.1 – 8.9  ·  Options A & B

Clause 8.1 · Options

Management system options

Option A (8.1.2)

The management system of a laboratory shall address the following:

  • Management system documentation (8.2)
  • Control of management system documents (8.3)
  • Control of records (8.4)
  • Actions to address risks and opportunities (8.5)
  • Improvement (8.6)
  • Corrective action (8.7)
  • Internal audits (8.8)
  • Management reviews (8.9)

Option B (8.1.3)

The laboratory has established and maintained a management system in accordance with ISO 9001:2015, and that is capable of supporting and demonstrating the consistent fulfilment of the requirements of Sections 4 to 7.

This also fulfils at least the intent of the management requirements specified in 8.2 to 8.9.

Assessor focus: Whichever option is chosen, the laboratory must still demonstrate the technical competence requirements of Clauses 4 to 7 in full.
Clause 8.2 · Management System Documentation

Management system documentation

  • 8.2.1 — Does the laboratory establish, document and maintain policies and objectives for the fulfilment of ISO 17025:2017 and ensure that these policies are acknowledged and implemented at all levels of the laboratory organisation?
  • 8.2.2 — Do the policies and objectives address the competence, impartiality and consistent operation of the laboratory?
  • 8.2.3 — How does the laboratory provide evidence that management is committed to the development and implementation of the management system and continually improving its effectiveness?
    To consider — ISO 9001:2015 clause 5.1:
    · Communicating to the organisation the importance of meeting customer as well as statutory and regulatory requirements
    · Establishing the quality policy
    · Ensuring that quality objectives are established
    · Conducting management reviews
    · Ensuring the availability of resources
  • 8.2.4 — Are all documentation, processes, systems, records, etc. included, referenced or linked to the management system? (To consider: ISO 9001:2015 clause 4.2.1)
  • 8.2.5 — Do all personnel involved in laboratory activities have access to the parts of the management system documentation and related information that are applicable to their responsibilities? (To consider: Document control, access rights to inspectors and contracted inspectors)
Clause 8.3 – 8.4

Control of management system documents & records

8.3 — Control of management system documents

Does the laboratory have procedures to control the documents (internal and external)? Does the laboratory ensure that:

  • a) Documents are approved for adequacy prior to issue by authorized personnel
  • b) Documents are periodically reviewed, and updated as necessary
  • c) Changes and the current revision status of documents are identified
  • d) Relevant versions of applicable documents are available at points of use and, where necessary, their distribution is controlled
  • e) Documents are uniquely identified
  • f) The unintended use of obsolete documents is prevented

8.4 — Control of records

  • 8.4.1 — Does the laboratory establish and retain legible records to demonstrate fulfilment of Section 8.4?
  • 8.4.2 — Does the laboratory implement the controls needed for the identification, storage, protection, back-up, archive, retrieval, retention time and disposal of its records? Is access to these records readily available and consistent with the confidentiality arrangements?
To consider: As a guide, important records should be kept at least through one accreditation cycle. (Ref: ISO 9001:2015 Clause 4.2.4)
Clause 8.5 – 8.6

Actions to address risks and opportunities & improvement

8.5 — Actions to address risks and opportunities

  • 8.5.1 / 8.5.2 — Does the laboratory consider the risks and opportunities associated with laboratory activities?
    • a) Does the laboratory plan actions to address these risks and opportunities?
    • b) How to: integrate and implement the actions into its management system; and evaluate the effectiveness of these actions?
  • 8.5.3 — Are the actions taken to address risks and opportunities proportional to the potential impact on the validity of laboratory results?
To produce: Records of identification of risks and opportunities and associated action plans.

8.6 — Improvement

  • 8.6.1 — Does the laboratory identify and select opportunities for improvement and implement any necessary actions?
  • 8.6.2 — Does the laboratory seek feedback, both positive and negative, from its customers? Does the laboratory analyse the feedback for purposes of improving the management system, laboratory activities and customer service?
Clause 8.7 · Corrective Action

Corrective action

8.7.1 — In the event of a nonconformity, does the laboratory:

  • a) React to the nonconformity and, as applicable:
    • Take action to control and correct it
    • Address the consequences
  • b) Evaluate the need for action to eliminate the cause(s) of the nonconformity, to prevent recurrence or occurrence elsewhere, by:
    • Reviewing and analysing the nonconformity
    • Determining the causes of the nonconformity
    • Determining if similar nonconformities exist, or could potentially occur
  • c) Implement any action needed
  • d) Review the effectiveness of any corrective action taken
  • e) Update risks and opportunities determined during planning, if necessary
  • f) Make changes to the management system, if necessary
  • 8.7.2 / 8.7.3 — Are the corrective actions appropriate to the effects of the nonconformities encountered?
To produce: Records of nonconformities, cause(s) and any subsequent actions taken · Records of the results of any corrective action.
Clause 8.8 · Internal Audits

Internal audits

  • 8.8.1 — Does the laboratory conduct internal audits to determine if the management system:
    • a) conforms to the laboratory's own requirements for its management system, including the laboratory activities?
    • b) conforms to the requirements of ISO 17025?
    • is effectively implemented and maintained?
  • 8.8.2 — Does the laboratory:
    • a) plan, establish, implement and maintain an audit programme including the frequency, methods, responsibilities, planning requirements and reporting — taking into consideration the importance of the laboratory activities concerned, changes affecting the laboratory, and the results of previous audits?
    • b) define the audit criteria and scope for each audit?
    • c) ensure that the results of the audits are reported to relevant management?
    • d) implement appropriate correction and corrective actions without undue delay?
    • e) retain records as evidence of the implementation of the audit programme and the audit results?
To produce: Records of internal audits.
Clause 8.9 · Management Reviews

Management reviews

  • 8.9.1 — Does the laboratory management review its management system to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy and effectiveness, including the stated policies and objectives related to the fulfilment of ISO 17025?
  • 8.9.2 — Does the laboratory record the following inputs to management review?
    • a) Changes in internal and external issues that are relevant to the laboratory
    • b) Fulfilment of objectives
    • c) Suitability of policies and procedures
    • d) Status of actions from previous management reviews
    • e) Outcome of recent internal audits
    • f) Corrective actions
    • g) Assessments by external bodies
    • h) Results of proficiency testing / inter-laboratory comparisons
    • i) Customer and personnel feedback
    • j) Complaints
    • k) Effectiveness of improvements implemented
    • l) Adequacy of resources
    • m) Results of risk identification
    • n) Outcomes of the assurance of the validity of results
    • o) Other relevant factors such as monitoring activities and training
  • 8.9.3 — Are the outputs of management review recorded, including decisions and actions related to improvement, resource needs and changes to the management system?
Annex A.3 · Nominee Assessment

Assessment of the nominee(s)

The technical assessor will evaluate the nominee(s) against the following questions:

No.Assessment Question
1Does the nominee possess the required technical qualification, experience and knowledge of the test/calibration methods?
2Does the nominee demonstrate competency in making critical evaluation of test/calibration results? (See requirements in A.3)
3Does/Do the nominee(s) spend sufficient time in the laboratory in order to exercise adequate supervision?
4Is/are the nominee(s) familiar with the quality system as documented in the quality manual and SINGLAS requirements?
5Comments from technical assessor on the nominee(s) technical qualification, experience, knowledge of test/calibration methods, competency in making critical evaluation of test/calibration results.
Key point: Nominees must be able to demonstrate both technical competence and familiarity with the management system — not just administrative presence.

Summary

Key takeaways

  • Clause 4 — Impartiality and confidentiality are ongoing obligations, not one-time declarations.
  • Clause 5 — The laboratory must be a legal entity with a defined, documented scope and clear responsibilities.
  • Clause 6 — Competent people, suitable facilities, calibrated equipment, unbroken traceability and controlled external providers.
  • Clause 7 — The technical process: valid methods, controlled records, sound uncertainty, valid results and defensible reports.
  • Clause 8 — A living management system: risks, improvement, corrective action, internal audits and management review.
  • Annexes — Nominee competence and the SAC obligations of an accredited laboratory.
